मात्रा अभ्यास – व

  • Write directly on the page with an Apple Pencil, S Pen or any active stylus.
  • 🖐️ Palm turns on pen-only mode so a resting hand draws nothing. It switches on by itself the first time a stylus is used.
  • 📏 shows or hides the four-line Hindi guides. Strong blue = शिरोरेखा, lighter line = writing line, dashed = how far matras may go.
  • 🧹 erases a whole stroke. ↶/↷ undo and redo. 🗑️ clears the current page only.
  • ⬇️ saves this page as a picture to hand in. 📦 saves every page written on.
  • Work is kept on this device and restored when the same browser is opened again.
  • Share → Add to Home Screen makes it open like an app, offline.
